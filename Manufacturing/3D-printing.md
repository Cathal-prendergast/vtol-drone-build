# 3D Printing

## Manufacturing Method

The majority of the aircraft airframe is manufactured using 3D printing.

3D printing was chosen because it allows complex aircraft components to be produced relatively quickly and makes it possible to modify individual parts during development.

## Printed Components

The 3D printed components include:

- Fuselage sections
- Wing components
- Tail components
- Motor mounts
- Other structural components

## Material

The primary material used is PLA because it provides a useful combination of strength, toughness and ease of printing.

PETG was going to be used, but there were structural and printing problems. PLA is used as a substitute instead.

## Manufacturing Approach

The airframe is divided into multiple sections so that the components can be manufactured within the printer's build volume.

Individual sections are assembled after printing.

## Design Considerations

The printed structure needs to be strong enough to withstand the loads experienced during flight while keeping the aircraft's mass as low as possible.

Print settings and wall thickness were therefore considered when designing the components.
